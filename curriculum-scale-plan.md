# TradeWise Academy — 2,000+ Lesson Architecture

## Scale and Structure

The curriculum expands the source-grounded **Stock Market Atlas** into a catalog of **2,016 short lessons**. The count is calculated as 84 reference topics across 14 domains multiplied by 24 distinct instructional frames per topic. Existing structured courses remain available as guided paths; the catalog is a complementary, searchable micro-learning library.

| Layer | Count | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Atlas domains | 14 | Organize the market system, products, disclosures, analysis, risk, and investor protection. |
| Reference topics | 84 | Supply the source-linked concept each lesson is anchored to. |
| Instructional frames | 24 | Turn every concept into definition, mechanism, evidence, risk, comparison, recall, and process-oriented lessons. |
| Catalog lessons | 2,016 | Deliver brief, individually addressable units without generating recommendations or trade signals. |

## Instructional Frames

The 24 frames are: orientation, definition, mechanism, actors, inputs, outputs, evidence, timing, comparison, trade-off, risk boundary, data caveat, disclosure lens, research question, scenario observation, misconception check, relationship map, metric lens, process checkpoint, volatility context, liquidity context, governance context, recall prompt, and synthesis.

Every generated lesson has a unique identifier; a title that combines its concept with its instructional frame; a short explanation grounded in that concept’s source; a guided prompt; a related-domain label; and a source attribution. The lesson template does not assert that a security should be bought, sold, held, or avoided.

## Quality Safeguards

The catalog preserves the Atlas’s source links and uses only concept-level framing. It intentionally omits live pricing, personalized allocation advice, security picks, forecasts, tax instructions, and real-money execution. Learner completion is persisted locally and kept separate from the existing core-course progress, so catalog study does not distort quiz accuracy for the guided curriculum.
