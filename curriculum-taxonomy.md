# TradeWise Academy — Comprehensive Reference Taxonomy

The expanded academy is designed as a structured learning library rather than an unbounded collection of market facts. The research inputs are limited to authoritative education and regulatory sources: **SEC and Investor.gov** for securities, public-company reporting, market mechanics, investor protection, and disclosures; **FINRA** for brokerage accounts, costs, margin, short selling, and investor-risk education; **OCC and the Options Industry Council** for options and clearing language; **Federal Reserve education** for rates, inflation, and monetary-system context; and **CFTC education** for futures, commodities, and derivatives boundaries.

| Library domain | Curriculum focus | Primary source lane |
|---|---|---|
| Securities foundations | Equity ownership, issuance, indexes, market pricing, primary and secondary markets | SEC / Investor.gov |
| Market plumbing | Brokers, exchanges, ATSs, market makers, clearing, settlement, custody, transfer agents | Investor.gov / SEC |
| Accounts and order execution | Cash and margin accounts, order types, fees, short selling, trade lifecycle | FINRA / Investor.gov |
| Company analysis | Business model, financial statements, filings, valuation questions, capital structure, corporate actions | SEC / Investor.gov |
| Fund and debt vehicles | ETFs, mutual funds, bonds, municipal securities, REITs, money-market funds, credit concepts | SEC / FINRA |
| Derivatives | Options vocabulary, basic payoff awareness, futures and commodity boundaries, leverage risks | OCC / CFTC |
| Market context | Rates, inflation, currency, sector and cross-asset context, macro uncertainty | Federal Reserve / Investor.gov |
| Governance and protection | Regulation, disclosures, fraud awareness, conflicts, suitability, risk discipline | SEC / FINRA |
| Practice and reflection | Synthetic scenarios, order rehearsal, journaling, review, decision-process habits | App-authored educational content |

The implementation will deliberately exclude real-time recommendations, personalized security selection, live trade signals, and individual tax, legal, or investment advice. Every simulator and scenario remains local, hand-authored, and synthetic.

## Next Atlas Expansion Domains

The next research and authoring set is deliberately bounded to six independent inputs: **SEC and Investor.gov** for public offerings, disclosure forms, ownership reports, and market-data literacy; **FINRA** for order handling, short interest, margin, and portfolio-risk education; **Federal Reserve** for international and financial-system context; **CFTC** for global derivatives and contract-risk boundaries; **SIPC** for account-protection distinctions; and **MSRB** for municipal-securities education. The resulting Atlas additions will cover securities issuance, ownership and governance, market data and benchmarks, portfolio measurement, global and cross-border market context, and account-protection concepts.
