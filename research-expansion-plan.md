# TradeWise Academy — Research Expansion Plan

## Objective

The next research pass expands the academy through **new operational and investor-protection angles**, not by repeating existing coverage of instruments, basic market structure, filings, portfolio metrics, or broad risk concepts. Each addition must be suitable for short educational lessons, have a primary or regulator-backed source lane, and avoid security-specific calls or personalized advice.

| Research lane | Coverage gap to address | Preferred source families |
|---|---|---|
| Trade operations | Order handling, trade reporting, auctions, halts, and post-trade lifecycle detail | SEC, FINRA, exchange investor education |
| Securities administration | Account transfers, proxy mechanics, record dates, restricted securities, and securities lending boundaries | SEC, FINRA, transfer-agent resources |
| Fund disclosure | Prospectus, shareholder reports, fee tables, money-market fund structures, and variable products | SEC / Investor.gov |
| Corporate restructurings | Tender offers, bankruptcy, delisting, going private, and debt-restructuring disclosures | SEC / Investor.gov |
| Financial stability | Payment and settlement systems, liquidity stress, banking-market links, and systemic-risk vocabulary | Federal Reserve, FSOC, SEC |
| Investor protection | Arbitration, complaints, account transfer process, fraud reporting, and due-diligence process | FINRA, SEC, SIPC |

## Integration Rules

New reference topics must include a concise definition, practical learning boundary, keywords, and a direct source URL. The existing 48 learning frames then turn each source-linked topic into multiple short lessons. Glossary additions must use neutral definitions and should distinguish terms that sound similar but have different legal, operational, or data meanings.

The resulting lessons should state what a learner can verify, which record or source provides context, and what uncertainty remains. They must not frame terminology or disclosures as an instruction to buy, sell, or hold a security.
